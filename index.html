About the agency

An independent agent for New York, New Jersey & Pennsylvania.

ND Agency LLC is a licensed independent insurance agent based in St. James, New York, serving clients across New York, New Jersey, and Pennsylvania. Because the agency isn't captive to a single insurance company, every policy is placed based on what actually fits your risk and your budget — not a script.

Quoting and writing a policy is always free. You pay the carrier's premium, and nothing extra to the agency for the advice or the paperwork.
